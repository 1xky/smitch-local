YOUR LIGHT, YOUR NETWORK

Bring your Smitch bulb back online.

Control compatible stock Smitch Wi-Fi bulbs directly from Android, Windows, macOS, or Linux. No account, cloud server, subscription, or modified bulb firmware.

Download Android APK Choose desktop version

Desktop requires Python 3.10+. Android requires Android 6.0+.

01

Fully local

Commands travel over your Wi-Fi directly to the bulb. The controllers do not need a Smitch login.

02

Keep stock firmware

No soldering or firmware flashing. Use the local protocol already built into compatible bulbs.

03

Cross-platform

A standalone Android app plus one lightweight browser controller for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

RECOMMENDED SETUP

Use your home Wi-Fi

Connect the bulb once to your 2.4 GHz router. After that, keep your phone or computer on its normal Wi-Fi and find the bulb locally.

  1. Join the bulb hotspot.Connect to the Wi-Fi network named Smitch.
  2. Send home Wi-Fi details.Use “Set up home Wi-Fi once” in either controller.
  3. Reconnect and discover.Return to home Wi-Fi, then find the bulb or enter its router IP.
